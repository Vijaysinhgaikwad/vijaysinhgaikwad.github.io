Automated correction workflows resolved identified metadata anomalies.

Outcome: Deployed a robust automated metadata intelligence system that surfaced previously inaccessible data attributes and validated their integrity systematically, significantly reducing manual QC dependency and improving the completeness and traceability of crash database records across national road safety programs.

Confidential Road Safety Analytics Web Portal

Objective: To design and deploy a secure, enterprise-grade web portal for managing confidential crash analytics, restricted investigation data, analytical dashboards, and role-based reporting access across authorized road safety program stakeholders.

Methodology: A Flask-based web architecture was engineered with multi-tier authentication and role-based access control systems enforcing data confidentiality at user, module, and dataset levels. Integrated analytical dashboards connected Power BI and Python-generated reports within the secured portal environment. SQL-backed data management workflows handled confidential crash record storage, retrieval, and update operations.

Outcome: Delivered a fully operational, secure enterprise analytics portal that centralized confidential crash intelligence, investigation data, and reporting workflows within a controlled, access-governed environment — providing authorized stakeholders with a professional, reliable platform for sensitive road safety program management and analytical oversight.
